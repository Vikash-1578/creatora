Creatora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Creatora is an Android app that helps creators plan, make and publish content and understand how their channels are doing. This page explains what the app collects and what it does with it.

What Creatora collects

  • Your Google account basics (name, email address, profile picture) when you sign in with Google.
  • Data from the accounts you connect (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Pages, TikTok): channel or profile details, your posts and videos, their statistics and analytics, and comments on them. Creatora only reads what each platform lets it read after you approve it on that platform's own sign-in screen.
  • What you create in the app: goals, plans, drafts, scripts, schedules and settings.
  • Crash and error reports, so problems can be fixed.

How it is used

  • To show your statistics, ideas, plans and reminders inside the app.
  • To publish or schedule a post to a connected account, only when you ask for it.
  • To produce AI suggestions. Text you send to the AI features, and the channel data needed to answer, is processed by Google's Gemini models through Firebase.

Creatora does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

  • Your profile, goals, plans and settings are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), under your own account.
  • Sign-in tokens for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok are stored encrypted on your phone and are not stored on Creatora's servers.
  • Crash reports are stored in Firebase Crashlytics.

Services Creatora relies on

Google Firebase and Google sign-in, YouTube API Services, the Instagram and Facebook APIs from Meta, and the TikTok API. Using Creatora with YouTube means you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect any account in Creatora's settings at any time.
  • You can remove Creatora's access from the platform itself: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram and Facebook under Settings, Apps and websites, and TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
  • You can ask for your Creatora data to be deleted by writing to the contact below. Deleting your account removes your stored profile, goals, plans and settings.

Children

Creatora is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions and deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Vikash-1578/creatora/issues.
